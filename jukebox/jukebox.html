About

Earth Jukebox turns NASA Earth data video into sound, so heat, cold and pollution can be heard as well as seen.

How it sounds

Each frame is shrunk to a grid of 64 columns and 14 rows, and every pixel is matched to the closest color on the track's legend to recover its value. Each row is a note, with the north high and the south low.

A line sweeps from west to east and plays the three columns under it, panned from your left ear to your right. On the temperature map, warm areas play a buzzing pad and cold areas ring like ice. Colors that are not on the legend, such as borders and labels, make no sound, and a quiet drone keeps calm regions from going silent.
