    About

    DANIG comes from two Tagalog words: daigdig, the world, and tinig, sound. Hence the motto: the world, through sound.

    DANIG turns visual data into soundscapes. It is a jukebox of preset videos from NASA’s Earth Information Center, each one a view of our changing planet, set to music that is easy on the ears. You can also upload your own video and color bar, then choose an instrument and a background scene.
