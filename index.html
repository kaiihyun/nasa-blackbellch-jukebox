    About

    This project turns global climate and weather videos into a soundscape you can explore as it plays. It reads the color of each video frame and turns it into sound.

    How it sounds

    Each frame is matched against its color bar to recover the data value underneath. At the listening spot you pick, that value becomes a nature sound: rain for precipitation, wind for land temperature, ocean swell for sea temperature. Brighter, more intense colors give fuller, louder textures.

    A musical note plays along with it. Pitch and volume rise as the value rises. When a place is warmer or wetter than usual, a second, slightly clashing note joins in.

    West sits in your left ear and east in your right. A low tone underneath tracks the whole planet. Areas with no signal are silent.
